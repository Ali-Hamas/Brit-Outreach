## Group Overview

Ascentra Global Ltd is a UK-registered group building **practical AI, talent and identity infrastructure for the modern enterprise**. We operate four complementary businesses under one parent, allowing us to serve clients from strategy through execution — from consulting and hiring to passwordless identity and cybersecurity.

**Team:** A vast, distributed team of engineers and delivery specialists across full-stack, AI/ML, DevOps, data, product and operations — available for client engagements (TalentBridge pool) and product development.

## Mission

To help organisations **transform through technology that is compliant, humane and economically sensible** — reducing cost, accelerating growth and protecting data without adding complexity.

## Vision

To become the **trusted infrastructure layer for AI transformation, remote talent and passwordless identity** across the UK and EU — where any organisation can adopt AI, scale a remote team, or let users sign up with a glance, with GDPR and eIDAS 2.0 built in.

## Core Principles

1.  **Business-first, tech-second** — outcomes over buzzwords.
2.  **Zero-knowledge & compliance by design** — GDPR, NHS DSP Toolkit, FCA, eIDAS 2.0.
3.  **Execution, not just advice** — we build, hire and run, not just consult.
4.  **Human-in-the-loop at critical gates** — automation qualifies and routes; a human confirms meetings and client agreements.

### 3. Biometric Sign Up — Technology (Separate from Sentrivault)
Biometric-only sign-up technology — unique identity number generated **single-device only** via on-device biometric (like "Sign up with Google"). For another device, user uses **email/password** (GDPR-compliant, encrypted, recoverable only via a user-generated safe code never stored in plaintext in the system). Server holds everything encrypted so breach does not affect user. Lucrative for **EU market** (eIDAS 2.0, GDPR-native, vendor-neutral).

**Offer:** Free early-access programme for traction.

**Target:** Gov orgs and app companies across **EU & UK** (EU priority) that need low-friction onboarding + compliance. Buyer: CISO, CTO, Product leads at app companies + gov decision makers. Trigger: wants passwordless, GDPR compliance, multi-device portability.

**Channel:** support@ascentraconsulting.co.uk | `crm_biometric_signup`

### 4. Sentrivault — Cybersecurity Product (Separate from Biometric Sign Up)
Enterprise cybersecurity infrastructure: **biometric authentication + end-to-end encrypted cloud storage** in one compliant layer. Built for GDPR/NHS DSP Toolkit/FCA. Separate product from Biometric Sign Up technology despite overlapping domain.

**Offer:** Free early-access / pilot programme.

**Target:** Orgs needing cybersecurity compliance; gov & regulated sectors (health, finance, legal, education, SaaS). Buyer: CISO, Head of InfoSec, DPO/Compliance, IT Directors.

**Channel:** support@ascentraconsulting.co.uk | `crm_sentravault` (separate profile from Biometric Sign Up despite same email domain)
